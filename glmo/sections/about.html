The GLMO is our first step towards a generic German labor market ontology. The GLMO includes both the ISCO and the KldB taxonomy to provide a generic framework for further data integration and mapping. For this reason, the GLMO is implemented as an extension of ESCO and Simple Knowledge Organisation System (SKOS). However, the key difference is a different scope and that we explicitly provide the mappings in one standardized format and provide further mappings and data, which are yet not available.

While ESCO describes three different domains (occupations; knowledge, skills and competences; qualifications), GLMO describes i) occupations, ii) skills, iii) tools and iv) industrial sectors. A fifth domain is added implicitly, the level of performance, which refers to both qualification and skill requirements for an occupation.
