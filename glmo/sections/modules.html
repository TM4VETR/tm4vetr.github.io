The GLMO comprises the following modules:
  • berufsschulen: Vocational schools.
  • gdr: Historical data from the former German Democratic Republic.
  • isco: International Standard Classification of Occupations (2008 version).
  • kldb: Klassifikation der Berufe (German occupational taxonomy; 2010 version).
  • kldb_1988: Klassifikation der Berufe (historical 1988 version).
  • kldb_1992: Klassifikation der Berufe (historical 1992 version).
  • kompetenzen: Competencies (DKZ lists and ESCO mappings).
  • weiterbildungen: Training programs.
  • wz: Economic sectors (Wirtschaftszweige; 2008 version).
For each module, a sub-ontology (.ttl file) is generated. Finally, these sub-ontologies are merged into the GLMO.
